Message Placement Study

We invite you to take part in a research study about when messages fit best in group conversations. You will read one group conversation and decide where each of three possible messages should be added, or whether it should not be added at all. The study should take less than 5 minutes.

Payment: You will receive $1.50 base pay upon completion of the study, plus a bonus of up to $2.00 based on how closely your choices match reference judgments.

Your responses will be used for research. The risks of participating are minimal. Participation is voluntary, and you may stop at any time.

By clicking "I agree" below, you confirm that you are 18 or older, are fluent in written English, and agree to participate in this study.

Your Task

You will work through ONE conversation and decide where to insert 3 different messages. Imagine you are participating in the conversation and find the best opportunity to send each message.

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Evaluate each message INDEPENDENTLY. One message should NOT affect whether you send another message.
  1. You will read the conversation one turn at a time
  2. After each turn, you'll see 3 message options on the right
  3. For each message, ask yourself independently: "Should I send THIS message now?"
    • If YES, click its "Send" button
    • If NO, just continue to the next turn
  4. You CAN send multiple messages in ONE turn if they all fit at that point
  5. Each message can only be sent once
  6. If you reach the end and a message never felt relevant, mark it as "Not Relevant"
